# How to Create a Record Company Deep Cuts Ecosystem

1. Give Codex the official record-company website URL.
2. Optionally provide an approved logo URL and a different notification email.
3. Codex starts **Build Record Company Deep Cuts**.
4. The system discovers and verifies the company and roster, creates every eligible page and quiz, checks every QR, publishes the collection, and emails the final package.

That is the complete operator process. There is no artist-by-artist approval step.

An artist that cannot pass the 98% identity and quiz-evidence gates is skipped and listed in the exception report. It does not stop the remaining roster.
