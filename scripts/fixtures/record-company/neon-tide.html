Neon Tide

Neon Tide formed in Melbourne and joined Midnight Harbour Records for the album Blue Static.

The group recorded Blue Static after building its arrangements from live room improvisations.

The album combines post-punk guitars with analogue synthesizers and was produced by Casey North.

The video for Harbour Lights was filmed overnight beside Port Phillip Bay.

The band’s first label release was the single Signal Fires.
