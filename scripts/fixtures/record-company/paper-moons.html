Paper Moons

Paper Moons are listed on Midnight Harbour Records.

Their five-piece arrangements pair chamber-pop strings with bright indie guitars.

The band recorded its label debut, Glass Houses, in a converted country hall.

Founding members Mara and Jo first performed together at a community radio fundraiser.

The single Lanterns introduced the album and its hand-built paper artwork.

Producer Avery Lane captured several performances live to tape.
