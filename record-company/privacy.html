Privacy

Deep Cuts records privacy-conscious usage events so record companies can understand discovery activity. These may include page views, QR scans, quiz activity, recommendations and outbound-link clicks.

A random session identifier is stored in your browser for the current session. The service may record device category and coarse country or region information supplied by the hosting platform. It does not store raw IP addresses, social login details or invasive device fingerprints.

Outbound destinations receive information according to their own privacy policies when you choose to open them. Analytics failure never prevents normal navigation.
